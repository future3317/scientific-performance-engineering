# Evolution Diagnostic Effect Closure

## Goal

Make the calibration diagnostics measure actual governed rule reuse and avoid
premature interaction decisions, while keeping ACRE promotion semantics and
confidence thresholds unchanged.

## Scope

1. Evolution episode transfer phases must include a same-family context inside
   the promoted predicate, plus an explicit counterexample and cross-family
   negative. Reuse metrics count only harness-routed intervention outcomes.
2. Factorial interaction diagnostics may stop only on canonical resolved
   decisions. `unresolved` and `underidentified_context_relation` remain open
   and consume the preregistered block schedule.
3. Boundary diagnostics honor the caller's surface horizon for every family
   and retain the FamilySpec-declared grammar capacity. StatisticalCEGIS and
   its gates are unchanged.
4. Evolution replay uses the versioned ``bounded_log_speedup_v1`` transform
   (``UTILITY_LOG_SCALE``) and collects up to three disjoint preregistered
   representative slices before synthesis. This addresses sparse positive
   strata without changing the promotion gate or exposing labels to the
   worker.
5. Router prompt cost is a soft penalty expressed as a fraction of the active
   context budget; the token budget remains a hard feasibility constraint.

## Non-goals

No changes to ACRE rule meaning, promotion thresholds, confidence-set
construction, router utility-LCB eligibility, benchmark scientific contracts,
or formal-50. The router's prompt-cost unit correction is in scope.

## Evidence requirements

- A positive transfer record must have `reused=True`, a non-empty deployed
  bundle, and a paired `delta` greater than the declared noise floor. If the
  conservative router still abstains because its valid utility-effect LCB is
  negative, the episode remains calibration-blocked rather than fabricating a
  reuse gain.
- A counterexample must remain in the episode raw record but cannot contribute
  to promotion or reuse utility.
- Interaction reports must retain the final scheduled estimate and report
  `stopping_blocks=None` when no canonical relation is identified.
- Boundary reports must preserve the sealed pool and report any remaining
  underidentified family as diagnostic-only.
- The compiler drift episode must show governed reuse on its matched dynamic
  transfer context, while Tiny/Recompile/Fusion remain abstentions. The
  observed calibration result is not a population efficacy claim.
